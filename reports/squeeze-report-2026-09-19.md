## 4. What changed

**Copper — the drain continues.** Cash−3M has gone from +535 (Aug 17 peak) to +14 (Sep 18), passing briefly negative in mid-September; LME stocks rose 32,125 t (+14.4%) over the 30-session window and 20,625 t (+8.8%) in the last week alone. Immediate-delivery scarcity is gone and the physical curve has flattened.

**Beef — a new flow print, no trend yet.** USDA's Cattle on Feed report (released Sep 18) gave the first placements/marketings/on-feed figures this data pipeline has captured (1.62M / 1.52M / 11.2M head); with no prior comparable reading, it establishes a baseline rather than a direction. Live and feeder cattle front-month futures are confirmed this week but also lack a comparable last-week figure.

**Gulf energy — transit up slightly, price signal muddled.** Hormuz daily transits ticked up from ~6/day (Sep 6) to 8/day (Sep 13, IMF PortWatch), still ~91% below the 85/day pre-crisis baseline. Separately, this week's lagged Brent spot print (EIA/FRED, $130.80, Sep 15) sits far above the front-month settlement ($99.29, Sep 18) — see Section 7 note below for why. Reports that Saudi Arabia moved several million bpd of crude around the Strait via Ras Tanura ship-to-ship transfers, and that Iran and Oman are close to a shipping-corridor agreement, are consistent with the front-month easing even while transit counts stay severely depressed.
