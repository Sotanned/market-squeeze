## 4. What changed

**Copper — premium up, stocks up, price down.** The cash−3M spread climbed from +26.5 (Sep 29) to +199 (Oct 30), but LME stocks, after draining to 225,425 t on Oct 16, jumped 27,675 t in one session on Oct 22 and finished at 274,000 t, +8.9% on the Sep 25 report. Cash fell 2.0% and 3M 2.7%. The three signals point different ways.

**Beef — placements rise.** The Oct 23 Cattle on Feed report shows placements 1.78M head vs 1.62M and on-feed 11.4M vs 11.2M. Marketings are flat at 1.52M. Front-month cattle futures are roughly flat (unlinked feed, see Data gaps).

**Cocoa — prices lower, contango narrower.** Front −2.7% and next −3.0% (unlinked feed); the linked spread narrowed to −104 from −124. Certified stocks remain unavailable.

**Gulf energy — price and freight up, flow data missing.** Brent front-month 113.43 (+16.4%) and tanker freight ETF 968.92 (+47.3%) rose while the drybulk ETF fell to 12.19 (−20.4%) (all three unlinked feeds). Linked Brent spot is 123.10 on Oct 27. PortWatch returned no Hormuz figure. Reporting through late October describes transits as slowly recovering but well below pre-war levels, with attacks continuing: Lloyd's List Intelligence counts at least 28 in October, and S&P Global reports six VLCC transits on Oct 25 and 17 ships on Oct 24–25 ([S&P Global](https://www.spglobal.com/energy/en/news-research/latest-news/crude-oil/102626-vlcc-transits-climb-at-hormuz-steady-at-bab-al-mandab-for-second-day), [Lloyd's List Intelligence](https://www.lloydslistintelligence.com/resources/blog/strait-of-hormuz-brief-22-october-2026), [UANI](https://www.unitedagainstnucleariran.com/node/46576)). Counts differ by source because each tracks a different vessel set; PortWatch cited 4 transits on Oct 18 ([straits.live](https://straits.live/briefs/2026-10-21)). None of these is a data-file figure.
