**Cocoa — prices up, curve flatter.** Front +1.5% and next +1.2%; contango narrowed from −124 to −109. Certified stocks are unavailable, so no physical read.

**Gulf energy — price and freight rise together.** Brent front-month rose 12.7% and the tanker ETF 36.7% while Hormuz transits sit at 2/day (Oct 11). Web search found no October-dated shipping report; the early-October coverage found attributes the Brent rise to renewed Hormuz and US–Iran uncertainty (Brent ~$102.30 on Oct 2) and cites Kpler crude flows near 13.2 million b/d, which disagrees with the PortWatch vessel count. Drybulk freight fell 18.1%, so the freight move is tanker-specific.
